I’m all about making stuff that hits differently. To me, coding isn't just about getting things to run—it's a craft. I love finding that sweet spot where logic meets human experience.

**My Approach:**

*   **Intentionality:** I’m a fan of quality over speed. I’d rather take the time to get it right.
*   **Balance:** I like to switch between deep technical work and creative projects to keep things interesting.
*   **Curiosity:** I’m obsessed with the "why" behind the code. If I can understand the fundamentals, everything else just clicks.

Whether I'm tweaking a UI or building out a backend, I’m always aiming to make things feel solid, thoughtful, and actually useful.
